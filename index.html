How it works

  1. Send an episode. A link to the video or the file.
  2. I pick the moments. AI helps me find candidates faster, but every clip is chosen and edited by me: a clear point, a strong first line, a clean ending.
  3. You get the clips in 48 hours. Captions burned in, vertical and square versions, a title for each. One round of changes included.

Fair questions

Why not an auto-clipping tool? Tools find loud moments. They don't know your audience, so you still spend an hour picking, fixing captions and writing posts. That hour is what I take off you.
