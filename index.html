05 /Questions

Fair questions

Why not an auto-clipping tool?

Tools find loud moments. They don't know your audience, so you still spend an hour picking, fixing captions and writing posts. That hour is what we take off your plate.

What happens after the free clips?

Nothing, unless you want more. Then you order one episode at €150 or start a monthly plan. We never bill without an order from you.

Who owns the clips?

You do. Pilot clips are yours to use straight away, paid clips once they are paid for. We use your episodes only to make your clips.

Will you show our clips anywhere?

Only with your written OK. That is what the founding-client price is for; otherwise it is simply your call.

Can you work on episodes before they air?

Yes. Unreleased episodes stay private. We delete the source files 30 days after delivery, unless you ask us to keep them longer.

What does AI see?

We transcribe the audio on our own computer. Only the transcript text goes to an AI model, which suggests candidate moments. A person picks and edits every clip and checks the captions.

Do you need access to our accounts?

No. We don't post for you and never ask for passwords. You get the files and post them yourself.

How does payment work?

By invoice, in euros. One episode is invoiced per episode; monthly plans are billed at the start of each month and due within 7 days.

What do you need from us?

A link to each new episode, and your brand colours and logo once.

Who is behind this?
