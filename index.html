Fair questions
Why not an auto-clipping tool?

Tools find loud moments. They don't know your audience, so you still spend an hour picking, fixing captions and writing posts. That hour is what we take off your plate.

Who owns the clips?

You do. We use your episodes only to make your clips.

A link to each new episode, and your brand colours and logo once.
